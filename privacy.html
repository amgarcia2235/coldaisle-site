Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Short version: Cold Aisle does not collect any data. There is no account, no server of ours, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you enter and what the app reads from your servers stays on your iPhone or iPad and travels only between it and the servers you add.

What the app stores, and where it stays

On your device only:

  • The servers you add: the name and tag you give them, their address and ports, and the user name.
  • Passwords, in the iOS Keychain, marked for this device only. They are not included in backups that can be restored to another device and they do not sync through iCloud Keychain.
  • For a server reached over Redfish, the fingerprint of its certificate, so the app can tell you if it ever changes.
  • The result of the last check of each server: its health, power state, the time, and the hardware summary the BMC reports (maker, model, serial number, firmware versions). For a server reached over IPMI, also its list of sensors, so the next check is quick.
  • Your settings.

Sensor readings and event log entries are shown while you look at them and are not kept. The app does not upload, sync or share any of this. Deleting the app deletes it from your device.

What the app connects to

  • Your servers' management controllers (BMCs), at the addresses you enter: over HTTPS for Redfish and over IPMI 2.0 (UDP port 623 unless you change it). Your user name and password are sent only to that address, and for Redfish only to the certificate you were shown when you added the server.
  • Apple, for purchases and to check what you have bought.

Nothing else. There is no relay and no server run by the developer. iOS asks for Local Network permission the first time the app reaches an address on your network; the app uses it only to reach the addresses you entered.

The widget and background refresh

The Home Screen widget shows the result of the app's last check, which the app saves on your device for it. The widget itself does not use the network or read passwords. If you turn on background refresh in Settings, the app can check your servers while it is closed; for that, saved passwords become readable while the phone is locked after its first unlock. This is off unless you turn it on.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your face, fingerprint or passcode and tells the app only whether it passed. The app never sees or stores biometric data.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud Backup or to your computer, that backup includes the list of your servers and your settings, under Apple's terms. Passwords are not restored to a different device.

Purchases

Cold Aisle Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; the developer never sees your payment details. Apple gives developers sales totals that do not identify you.

Diagnostics from Apple

The app contains no crash reporting or analytics code of its own. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give the developer crash reports and usage totals that do not identify you.

Compatibility reports

A server's Connection screen can make a short compatibility report: the maker and model of the board, its BMC and BIOS firmware versions, the protocol in use and which features work. It leaves out the server's name, address, ports, user name, serial number, host name, certificate and password. The app shows you the text and hands it to the iOS share sheet; it never sends it anywhere by itself. If you send it to the developer, for example in a support issue, it is used only to make the app work with that kind of board.

Demo servers

The built-in demo servers are simulated inside the app. Using them makes no network connection.

Children

The app is a tool for people who run servers and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
